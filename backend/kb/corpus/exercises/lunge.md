# Walking Dumbbell Lunge

Unilateral lower body exercise training quadriceps, adductors, glutes, and dynamic balance.

## Execution
Take a controlled step forward, lower back knee toward ground until both knees reach approximately 90 degrees, and push back up through the front heel.

## Regressions
Regress to stationary reverse lunges or split squats supported by a TRX strap for members with patellar irritation.
