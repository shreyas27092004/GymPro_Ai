# Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press

Vertical pressing movement building anterior and lateral deltoids, triceps, and clavicular pectorals.

## Execution
Sit upright with back supported, press dumbbells vertically until arms are extended overhead without arching the lower back excessively.

## Regressions
Regress to landmine angled press if vertical overhead reach causes pinching in the acromion space.
