# Conventional Deadlift

A primary posterior chain exercise building hip hinge strength, glutes, hamstrings, and upper back stability.

## Execution
Stand with feet hip-width apart, grip bar outside shins, pull slack out of bar, drive feet into the floor keeping bar close to shins, and extend hips.

## Regressions
Regress to elevated trap bar deadlift or Romanian deadlift with dumbbells to minimize lumbar torque.
