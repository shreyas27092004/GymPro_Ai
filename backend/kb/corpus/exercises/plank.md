# Forearm Plank

Isometric core endurance exercise strengthening the anterior core, glutes, and scapular stabilizers.

## Execution
Support body on forearms and toes, maintain neutral cervical and lumbar alignment, squeeze glutes and brace core. Hold for prescribed duration without letting hips sag.

## Regressions
Regress to knee plank or incline plank with forearms on an elevated surface to reduce shoulder lever-arm strain.
