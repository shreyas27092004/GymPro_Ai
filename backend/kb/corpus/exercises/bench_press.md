# Barbell Bench Press

Compound horizontal pressing movement targeting the pectoralis major, anterior deltoid, and triceps brachii.

## Execution
Lie on bench with eyes under bar, retract and depress scapulae, grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width, lower bar smoothly to mid-chest, and press to lockout.

## Regressions
Regress to neutral-grip dumbbell press on flat bench or floor press to reduce shoulder impingement stress.
