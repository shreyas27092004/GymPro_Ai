# Standard Push-up

Calisthenics horizontal press challenging chest, triceps, and anterior core stability.

## Execution
Form a straight line from heels to head, hands slightly outside chest width. Lower chest until 2 inches from floor, tucking elbows at 45 degrees, and push back up.

## Regressions
Regress to hands-elevated incline push-ups on a box or bench, or use push-up handles to keep wrists neutral.
