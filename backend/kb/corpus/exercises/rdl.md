# Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

A knee-friendly posterior chain developer emphasizing hamstring stretch and eccentric hip flexion control.

## Execution
With soft knees, push hips backward horizontally while sliding dumbbells down along thighs. Stop when torso reaches 45 degrees or hamstrings feel full tension.

## Regressions
Regress to bodyweight good mornings or floor single-leg glute bridges if back fatigue presents.
