# Russian Kettlebell Swing

Ballistic hip hinge power movement conditioning glutes, hamstrings, and cardiovascular endurance without knee impact.

## Execution
Hinge at hips with bell between legs, snap hips into full extension to propel bell to chest level. Arms act solely as guides; power originates from hip drive.

## Regressions
Regress to banded pull-throughs or Romanian deadlifts if the ballistic snap strains the lower back.
