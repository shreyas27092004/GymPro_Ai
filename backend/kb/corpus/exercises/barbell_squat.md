# Barbell Back Squat

A fundamental compound lower body movement developing quadriceps, gluteus maximus, and spinal erector strength.

## Execution
Maintain a neutral spine, brace your core, descend by breaking at the hips and knees simultaneously until hip crease is parallel with knees, then drive through mid-foot.

## Regressions
For knee discomfort or mobility limits, regress to a goblet squat seated to a 16-inch box.
