# Plyometric Box Jump

Explosive vertical jump movement developing rate of force development in triple extension (ankles, knees, hips).

## Execution
Dip into quarter squat, swing arms forward, jump powerfully onto box landing softly in athletic squat position. Step down carefully one foot at a time. Never rebound backwards.

## Regressions
Regress to alternating high-tempo step-ups or broad jumps onto soft mats. Contraindicated for knee or ankle conditions.
