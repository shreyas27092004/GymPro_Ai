# Free Trial Terms & Entitlements

New registered members receive a complimentary trial pass upon email verification to experience GymPro facilities and group classes.

## Terms
- Allowance: Exactly 3 group class passes valid for 14 calendar days from registration date.
- Eligibility: Strictly once per person, verified by email and encrypted phone hash. Duplicate accounts cannot claim additional trial classes.
- Exclusions: Personal training sessions and premium workshops are excluded from trial passes.
- Expiration: Any unused trial credits expire at 23:59 on day 14.
