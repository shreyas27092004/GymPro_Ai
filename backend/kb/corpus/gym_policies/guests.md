# Guest Access & Facility Policy

GymPro welcomes members to bring guests under the following terms:
- Gold members receive 1 complimentary guest pass per calendar month.
- Platinum members receive 3 complimentary guest passes per calendar month.
- Guests must sign a liability waiver and complete a basic health screening at the reception desk before using the gym floor.
- Guests must be accompanied by the sponsoring member at all times.
