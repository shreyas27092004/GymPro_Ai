# Membership Refund & Billing Disputes Policy

GymPro maintains transparent billing and refund terms for all digital purchases and recurring subscriptions.

## Refund Rules
- Cooling-Off Period: New monthly or annual memberships can be cancelled within 72 hours of purchase for a full refund minus a 500 INR administrative fee, provided no classes were booked.
- Prorated Upgrades: Upgrades take effect immediately with proration applied as a credit. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle and are processed by staff.
- Billing Disputes: Any disputed charge submitted via the AI coach or support console is reviewed by the finance administration team within 2 business days. AI agents cannot issue or promise immediate cash refunds.
