# Lower Back Pain Management Protocol

Lumbar spine irritation requires limiting spinal flexion under axial load, high velocity torsional movements, and uncontrolled hyperextension.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Heavy barbell good mornings, conventional deadlifts from floor with flexion, weighted crunches, kettlebell swings.
- Preferred: Bird-dogs, McGill side planks, dead bugs, gentle cat-cow transitions, supported goblet squats.
- Focus: Neutral spine endurance, hip flexor extensibility, transverse abdominis bracing.
