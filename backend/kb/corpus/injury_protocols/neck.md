# Cervical Spine & Neck Strain Protocol

Cervical stiffness or nerve impingement requires avoiding heavy axial compressions, extreme cervical extension, and inverted yoga postures.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Behind-the-head pulldowns, headstands, plow pose, high-load barbell shrugs, bridging on crown of head.
- Preferred: Chin tucks, thoracic extensions on foam roller, band pull-aparts, gentle neck isometrics.
- Focus: Deep cervical flexor endurance, upper trapezius relaxation, scapular posture.
