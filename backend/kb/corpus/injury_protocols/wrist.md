# Wrist Strain & Carpal Compression Protocol

Wrist extension deficits or tendonitis contraindicate loaded hyperextension during pressing, cleans, and floor push-ups.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Floor push-ups on flat palms, heavy clean & jerk front rack, kettlebell front rack on back of wrist.
- Preferred: Dumbbell presses with neutral grip handles, push-up bars, machine chest press with neutral grip.
- Focus: Forearm flexor/extensor balance, wrist roller isometric holds.
