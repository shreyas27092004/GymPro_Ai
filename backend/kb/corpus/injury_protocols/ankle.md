# Ankle Sprain & Instability Protocol

Lateral ankle sprains or Achilles tendinopathy require avoiding sudden changes of direction, lateral bounding, and high-impact barefoot training.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Box jumps, double-unders, sprint drills, aggressive lateral shuffles.
- Preferred: Stationary indoor cycling, recumbent ergometer, seated strength work, controlled balance pad work.
- Focus: Peroneal strengthening, calf eccentric drops, ankle dorsiflexion mobility.
