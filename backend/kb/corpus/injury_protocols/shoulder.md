# Shoulder Impingement & Rotator Cuff Protocol

Shoulder joint restrictions (subacromial impingement, rotator cuff tendinopathy) contraindicate loaded vertical overhead pressing and deep anterior shoulder stretches.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Behind-the-neck barbell presses, deep parallel bar dips, upright barbell rows, wide-grip bench press.
- Preferred: Landmine neutral presses, external rotations with light resistance bands, face pulls, chest-supported rows.
- Focus: Scapular upward rotation, serratus anterior recruitment, thoracic spine mobility.
