# Knee Injury Management Protocol

Members experiencing acute or chronic knee pain (such as patellar tendonitis, meniscus irritation, or ligament strain) must avoid repetitive high-impact loading, deep uncontrolled flexion, and rapid decelerations.

## Recommended Modifications
- Avoid: Jump squats, box jumps, sprints, lunges with knee tracking beyond toes, high-impact aerobics, HIIT.
- Preferred: Low-impact cycling with moderate resistance, swimming, mat pilates, gentle restorative yoga.
- Focus: Quadriceps isometric strengthening, hamstring and calf mobility, gluteus medius activation.
