# Fluid & Electrolyte Balance Protocol

Dehydration exceeding 2% of body mass significantly impairs cognitive focus, aerobic endurance, and anaerobic power output.

## Baseline Recommendations
- Baseline fluid intake: 35 ml per kg of body mass per day.
- Exercise sweat compensation: 500 to 750 ml per hour of vigorous training, adjusted for ambient temperature and sweat rate.
- Electrolytes: Sodium supplementation (300-500 mg per liter) is recommended for sessions exceeding 60 minutes in hot or humid environments.
