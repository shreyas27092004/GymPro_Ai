# Food Composition & Protein Sources

Optimal recovery relies on high-quality complete proteins providing all essential amino acids (EAAs) with sufficient leucine (2.5 to 3.0 g per serving).

## Food Categories
- Animal Sources: Chicken breast, eggs, Greek yogurt, whey protein isolate, salmon. High bioavailability (DIAAS > 1.0).
- Plant Sources: Tofu, tempeh, lentils, edamame, pea and rice protein blends. Complementary pairings ensure complete amino acid profiles.
- Allergen Awareness: Common gym food allergens include peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, soy, eggs, and shellfish. Strict elimination is required when allergy flags are present.
