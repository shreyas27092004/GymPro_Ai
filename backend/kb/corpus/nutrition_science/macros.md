# Macronutrient Distribution & Energy Balance

Evidence-based nutritional science dictates that total energy balance governs weight change, while macronutrient distribution dictates body composition preservation and athletic performance.

## Energy Densities
- Protein: 4 kcal per gram. Target 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of body mass for active resistance-trained individuals.
- Carbohydrate: 4 kcal per gram. Primary glycolytic fuel source for high-intensity training.
- Fat: 9 kcal per gram. Crucial for hormone synthesis, cell membrane integrity, and fat-soluble vitamin absorption (minimum 0.6 to 1.0 g per kg).
